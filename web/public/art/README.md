## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production background artwork for a monochrome productivity app, landscape 1536 by 1024. Primary request: a monumental classical marble portrait dominating a black canvas, styled as a sophisticated high-contrast halftone / ordered-dither print. Subject: an original antique Greek marble bust of a calm young adult with sculpted short curls, in a strong left-facing three-quarter profile, cracked weathered stone, long neck and hint of shoulder. Composition: the enormous face and neck occupy the RIGHT 65 to 70 percent of the canvas, extend from close to the top to the bottom edge, with some back-of-head and shoulder cropped by the right and bottom edges. Face looks inward left. The LEFT 30 percent is pure deep black negative space for application text; the dark chest and lower neck dissolve naturally into black. This is a background integrated underneath UI, not a small centered illustration. Lighting: dramatic carved highlights from upper left, face strongly lit, far cheek in absolute shadow. Medium: tiny crisp black-and-white stipple dots and halftone pixels, extremely detailed stone texture and engraved sculptural curls, sharp silhouette. Palette: neutral near-white and grays on pure black, no colored light, no orange, no blue. Constraints: ARTWORK ONLY. Absolutely no text, typography, letters, numbers, UI panels, frames, symbols, diagrams, logos, interface controls, circles, or watermarks. No Sisyphus or other full-body scene. The portrait should be large, quiet, imposing, and beautifully legible on a black background.
